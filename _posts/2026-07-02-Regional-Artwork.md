# Solution

As is often the case with Jane Street Puzzles, my first instinct was to check previous puzzles in search of similarities.
After a few minutes, I found one: [Hidden Warning](https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/the-hidden-warning-index/) from March 2022.
After reading the solution, I had a decent idea of what I was looking for.

I also took a moment to look at the bigger picture and noticed that there were 14 clues, and the final clue seemed to have 14 letters. The puzzle mentioned sorting, so I assumed it would be alphabetical.

Off the bat, I thought I knew the answers to some of the clues:

- "frequent Frazier foe" = `Ali`
- "eponym of multiple continents" = `Vespucci` (the letter "V" also comes near the end of the alphabet)
- "notable 2026 launch" = `Artemis`
- "the present, in the past" = `the future`
- "our nearest neighbors, in some sense (though we haven't visited yet)" = `Mars` or `Alpha Centauri`

The internet helped come up with some more answers (and clarified Artemis):

- "stage name of a famous skeptic" = `The Amazing Randi`
- "where to find "take thou this vial" and "double, double toil and trouble" = `Act IV Scene I`
- "onetime Outkast output" = `Speakerboxxx`, `Stankonia`, or `Aquemini`
- "notable 2026 launch" = `Artemis II`

The third—and most important—clue eluded me, though. I considered `Artificial Intelligence`, `Climate Change`, things related to geopolitics or even computer hardware, but was stuck for like a week.

Later, after figuring out that "type of magazine you might start getting in your 20's" referred to `Alumni`, I finally noticed that `Ali`, `Alumni`, `Artificial`, `Amazing`, `Amerigo`, and `Artemis` started with the letter "A"—it seemed too unlikely to be a coincidence. 
From there, it didn't take long to figure out that the third clue was `AI`, and the answers had to start and end with "A" and "I," respectively.

Around this time, I also realized that the answers also had lengths 2–15. 
This helped with the rest of the clues, though there were certainly some difficulties.
The clue "the present, in the past" was particularly difficult. While I was able to solve it, I still don't exactly understand how it fits.

After solving all the clues, the sorting was pretty straightforward, and "George Lucas Hit" was presented as the final clue.
Going through the Wikipedia page for [George Lucas Filmography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas_filmography), I remembered how the answer to the 2022 puzzle was related to the title.
With that in mind, Oscar-nominee `American Graffiti` was the obvious answer.

Overall, I thought the puzzle was fun, but some of the clues were very frustrating.
I know that some of the other solvers thought this was an easy puzzle, but I found it quite challenging.
Admittedly, that may be, in part, due to my age; I am not yet an alumnus of high school.
